# Research Standards

## Research types

| Type | Purpose | Timeline |
|------|---------|----------|
| Discovery | Understand problems and needs | 1-3 weeks |
| Validation | Test specific solutions | 1-2 weeks |
| Usability | Evaluate existing flows | 3-5 days |
| Survey | Quantitative patterns at scale | 1 week |

## Interview process

1. Define research question (what do you need to learn?).
2. Write a discussion guide (open-ended questions, no leading).
3. Recruit 5-8 participants per segment.
4. Record and transcribe sessions.
5. Synthesize with affinity mapping.
6. Present findings as themes + quotes + recommendations.

## Documentation

- One-pager per study: question, method, participants, findings, recommendations.
- Store all raw data (notes, recordings, transcripts) in a shared research repository.
- Tag findings by theme so they accumulate over studies.

## Integrating research

- Share findings within 48 hours of synthesis.
- Connect research insights to specific roadmap items.
- Revisit past research before starting new studies — avoid duplicate effort.
