# Sprint Planning Standards

## Sprint cadence

- 2-week sprints. Start on Monday, end on Friday.
- Planning on Monday morning. Retro on Friday afternoon.
- Sprint goal: one sentence describing what the team will achieve.

## Backlog refinement

- Refine backlog items at least one sprint ahead.
- Every item must have: title, description, acceptance criteria, estimate.
- Use story points or T-shirt sizing — be consistent within the team.
- Maximum item size: half a sprint. Break larger items into smaller ones.

## Sprint planning meeting

- Review the sprint goal proposal.
- Select items from the top of the backlog that support the goal.
- Verify capacity (vacations, on-call, meetings).
- Each team member commits to specific items.

## During the sprint

- Daily standup: what done, what next, any blockers. Max 15 minutes.
- Update ticket status as work progresses. Board reflects reality.
- Scope changes mid-sprint: discuss in standup, adjust sprint goal if needed.

## Retro

- What went well / What to improve / Action items (max 3).
- Action items must have an owner and a deadline.
- Review previous retro action items at the start.
