# Competitive Analysis Skill

## When to use

- Evaluating a market opportunity.
- Understanding competitive positioning.
- Benchmarking against alternatives.

## Workflow

```
1. DEFINE   — What question are you answering? Who are the competitors?
2. RESEARCH — Gather data from public sources, reviews, documentation.
3. ANALYZE  — Compare across structured dimensions.
4. SYNTHESIZE — Extract insights, not just facts.
5. PRESENT  — Share findings with actionable recommendations.
```

## Analysis dimensions

| Dimension | What to capture |
|-----------|----------------|
| Features | Core capabilities, differentiators, gaps |
| Pricing | Tiers, models (per-seat/usage), free tier |
| Target market | Segment, geography, company size |
| Tech stack | Open/closed source, integrations, APIs |
| GTM motion | Sales-led, product-led, channel |
| Momentum | Funding, growth rate, hiring, press |

## Research sources

- Product documentation and pricing pages.
- G2, Capterra, Gartner reviews.
- Job postings (reveal priorities and tech stack).
- Conference talks and blog posts.
- Patent filings for deep tech analysis.

## Output format

```markdown
# Competitive Analysis: [Topic]

## Summary
[2-3 sentence executive summary]

## Market landscape
[Overview of the space and key players]

## Competitor profiles
[One section per competitor with the dimensions above]

## Comparison matrix
[Table comparing key features/metrics across competitors]

## Opportunities
[Gaps in the market that represent opportunities]

## Risks
[Competitive threats and how to mitigate]
```

## Style

Prose output — Summary, market landscape, competitor profiles, opportunities — write
in informational style per the `infostyle` skill: fact over opinion, strong-position
sentences, one-thought paragraphs. Bedrock (stop-words, fact-over-opinion) in
`_shared/infostyle-core.md`.
