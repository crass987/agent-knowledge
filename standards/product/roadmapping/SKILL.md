# Roadmapping Standards

## Roadmap structure

- Organize by quarters with clear themes (not feature lists).
- Each initiative must state: what, why (business value), and success metric.
- Maintain a backlog for unprioritized ideas. Review quarterly.

## OKRs

- 3-5 objectives per quarter, each with 2-4 measurable key results.
- Key results must be quantifiable: "Increase DAU from 10K to 15K," not "Improve engagement."
- Distinguish leading indicators (predictive) from lagging indicators (outcome).

## Prioritization

- Use RICE (Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort) or similar framework.
- Document the reasoning behind priority decisions, not just the rank.
- Re-evaluate priorities when new data arrives, not just at planning sessions.

## Communication

- Share roadmap with stakeholders at two levels: executive summary (themes + timelines) and detailed (initiatives + metrics).
- Update roadmap status bi-weekly. Flag blockers and scope changes immediately.
- Keep a "decisions log" — what was considered, what was chosen, and why.
