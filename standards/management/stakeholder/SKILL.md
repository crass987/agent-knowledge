# Stakeholder Communication Standards

## Update cadence

| Audience | Format | Frequency |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Executive | 1-page summary with key metrics | Monthly |
| Cross-functional | Status email or Slack update | Bi-weekly |
| Team | Sprint review / demo | Per sprint |
| Individual | 1:1 with action items | Weekly |

## Status updates

- Lead with impact: what changed for users/business, not what the team did.
- Use the format: Done / In progress / Blocked / Next.
- For blockers: state the blocker, who can resolve it, and the deadline.

## Presenting data

- Always include the time range and comparison (vs. last period, vs. target).
- Show trends, not just snapshots. Sparklines > tables.
- Flag anomalies with context: "DAU dropped 15% — correlated with payment gateway outage."

## Escalation

- Escalate early, not when it's too late to course-correct.
- Include: what's at risk, the impact, what's been tried, and what you need.
- Propose solutions, don't just report problems.
